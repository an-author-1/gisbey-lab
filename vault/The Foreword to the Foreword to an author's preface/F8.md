However, an important distinction should clearly be made. Salinger was reclusive by choice, and it would appear that Malt Gibsey most certainly was not.

Though also, things are not always as they appear, and as we face this era outside of time—if time outside of time can even thus be called an “era”—one can’t help but wonder if Malt Gibsey is still with, even if only in spirit.

What this spirit is exactly will be up to the reader to decide, and whether or not that itself is a subversive, or Subversionist, question to consider can instead be taken up by Jacklyn Variance in documents such as Problems of an author’s Poetics, a redundant, rambly, overblown, and needlessly digressive text if there ever was one.
