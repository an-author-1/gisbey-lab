“Well, it broke the formula. Mostly. He doesn’t leave. But what do you want to do?”

“I keep sending them the same story, and I get rejected every time. This is one of the only versions we’ve made together where he stays. Maybe that’s enough to send, but I don’t want to.”

“What do you want to do?”

“There are plenty of other things I’d prefer doing.”

“Oh, are there?”

They exit the car, walk up the stairs through the puddles and columns of wet falling down upon them.

And pass, fully, safely, into the inside of their apartment building.

Into a mirror of a mirrored room, with the door closed behind them.
