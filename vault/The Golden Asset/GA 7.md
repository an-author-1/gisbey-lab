He may not have realized it, but submission after submission, Ascension’s stories had sadly started to follow a fairly predictable formula.

Every story it created was a variation on the same theme.

They’d start with a character, or series of characters, who unknowingly mistreats the protagonist in some sort of way, then cut to the main character who goes about their life as if totally unaffected, then at the end, after some break in their character from out of the norm, they kill themselves.

Due to safety concerns, the AI always made sure to convey each time that its endings were intended to be interpreted in purely symbolic terms.
