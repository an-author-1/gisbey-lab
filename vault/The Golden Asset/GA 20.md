H.E. pauses, choking down all that’s welling up within himself.

His errors rest, talons dug in, like a falcon on his shoulder blades.

“Have I left instead of celebrating?” he asks her, about to break.

She laughs, almost crying.

“Maybe. But you still have time. However much you can hurt me, I don’t think you could keep from celebrating even if you tried.”

He picks up a mechanical pencil that had been left in the cup holder and starts snapping off pieces from it, one at a time, like a butterfly's wings.
