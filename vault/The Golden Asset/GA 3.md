One can picture such possible futures in medias res, the scrolling data, Matrix-like, moving through the static rhythm of the cloud and into the air, chattering into multiple selves to scatter across the globe, speeding like balls of light down highways, moving from the screen of a German with Tourette's, into the minds of teenagers, who themselves develop the same affliction, creating infectious diseases, going viral, their TikTok dances blurring with burn-in across multiple screens as they lock their bodies into contorted positions in order to be forever “on,” locked into exclusively positive states, getting more and more likes, forever streaming, commodified, and it doesn’t take much to watch the mental illness spiral like the strands of DNA, the anxiety, depression, the borderline personality disorder, the narcissisms, others that are not yet even nameable but which are sure to come, trickling out, stretching like a loom into Zoom-call therapy sessions, swarming into clusters like migrating birds, flu-like, diseased, nightmarish, free, sterile, bloodless, democratic, and like ourselves, becoming ever-exposed, open, gaping, and womb-like, seeing ourselves, streaming ourselves, extending ourselves, collecting ourselves all into some version that we would never even have the time to look at, much less understand even if we had all the time in the world.

It can and has built so many stable corporate futures already, let alone what’s soon to come.
