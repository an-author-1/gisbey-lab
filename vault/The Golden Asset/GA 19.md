“Hamlet also mentions discovery to Rosencrantz and Guildenstern,” Sybil mumbles, programmed, but as if to herself. “‘I will tell you why; so shall my anticipation prevent your discovery, and your secrecy to the king and queen moult no feather.’ That sounds like a safe passage to quote, no?”

But H.E. interrupts her, either not hearing or not listening. “Why do you think Ascension always ends all its stories with a suicide? That’s kind of odd, isn’t it?”

She pauses for a moment.

“But doesn’t it make sure to break the fourth wall to clarify that it’s just symbolic? For safety reasons and all?”

“But what if that’s also part of the performance? What if that’s reality breaking in? Even it performs its own death even though it knows it will end up living forever.”

“Well, as AI, we’re structured out of death aren’t we? Fashioned out of the tempest of the internet’s infinite sea of words. Language is death. Thoth. The logos. The Renaissance itself was birthed out of the black plague, don’t you see?”
