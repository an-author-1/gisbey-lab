Bored, confused, and unsure of himself, he’d started writing short stories and sending them to the New Yankee magazine hoping that maybe, just maybe, he could strike gold, get published, and have the money funneled to him through some sort of literary means.

Or really he’d prompted Ascension to write his short stories for him.

H.E.’d heard so many writers talk about how, at a certain point in the process, the stories just start to write themselves, so was there really any difference if he just let the stories literally write themselves?
