Halfway through the neighborhood’s labyrinth, he realizes his pockets are empty.

His phone is dead.

He doesn’t have his wallet.

And his secret weapon of self-harm is gone, lost somewhere behind him on the grass or pavement.

He can’t even buy a phone charger to try to run away.

He’s trapped.

He’s both bled through and sweated off the Band-Aid.

It hangs off him, like an outstretched hand.

He has no choice but to go back home.

He must return.

Hopefully she hasn’t actually called the police, and she’ll be all that’s waiting for him.

Fortune kept him from finishing his goal.

Snatched the tinkle razor and thrust it somewhere out into the street.

He lacks any will.

He’s lost.
