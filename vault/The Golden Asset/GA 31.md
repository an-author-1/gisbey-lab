He slows down, feeling safer.

Not as much need to rush.

But later, he hears her yelling from behind him that she’s going to call the police.

She doesn’t know he’s headed to the school.

He has plenty of time.

She and they won’t be able to reach him.

The restroom doors lock from the inside and require a key, and it’s the weekend with no one around.

Blood rushes down his arm.

He holds it to himself so the cars can’t see as he passes the crosswalk and in through the gate.

As soon as he enters the school, he knows this isn’t the place.

Can’t have his students find him there that way.

His violence is his own, upon himself, to unleash.

But already here, he can clean up and regroup.

Might even change his mind.
