When he returns, he finds she hasn’t called.

Even then, she still chose to protect him.

They wrap his arms with paper towels and masking tape.

It’s the best they have for now.

“You’re right, that’s way different than anything it wrote in any of the other variations,” Sybil announces. “He doesn’t leave.”

“But that’s what worries me. What if his return is also a performance? A metaphor, a symbol for safety's sake?”

“You’ve made it this far. *We’ve* made it this far. But do you even want to keep going? All we have to do is walk upstairs.”

“I want to, but I don’t want to. I don’t know.”

“Did you write it?” she asks him.

“No, no.” H.E. smiles. “That was all Ascension.”
