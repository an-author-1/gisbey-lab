He thrusts his back against the locked bathroom door, propping his legs up against the sink to keep it closed behind him.

It doesn’t lock properly.

And he knows she’ll soon realize where he is.

She’s hidden all the knives.

He’s already tried it a few times.

More than he’s even told her or she’s discovered.

Plenty of times he’s just held a blade to his wrists to see if he even could, to see how close he may be able to bring himself to.

To test himself.
